## Q: What are your shipping options and costs?
A: We offer Standard shipping (3-5 business days, $4.99, free on orders over
$45) and Express shipping (1-2 business days, $9.99) within Australia. All
orders are shipped via Australia Post and include tracking.

## Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard shipping typically arrives within 3-5 business days of dispatch.
Express shipping arrives within 1-2 business days. Delivery times can be
longer for regional/remote postcodes. Dispatch itself usually happens within
1 business day of the order being placed, except during high-volume sale
periods.

## Q: What is your return policy?
A: Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and
in original condition. Once we receive and inspect the returned item,
refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
Digital/eBook purchases are non-returnable once downloaded.

## Q: How do I return a book?
A: Contact support with your order number to start a return. Once approved,
we'll email you a prepaid return shipping label. Pack the item securely and
drop it at any Australia Post outlet. You'll get a confirmation email once
we receive it, and again once your refund is processed.

## Q: Do you offer exchanges instead of refunds?
A: Currently we only offer refunds, not direct exchanges. If you'd like a
different title, return the original for a refund and place a new order.

## Q: My order arrived damaged or is missing items. What do I do?
A: We're sorry about that — this qualifies for a full refund or replacement
at no cost to you, and doesn't require the item to be sent back in most
cases. Contact support with your order number and, if possible, a photo of
the damage, and we'll prioritize resolving it.

## Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the Sign In page and click "Forgot password?", then enter the
email on your account. We'll send a password reset link valid for 60
minutes. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam
folder. If you're chatting with support, we can trigger this reset email
for you directly.

## Q: How do I check my order status?
A: You can check order status by signing into "My Orders" on our website,
or by giving support your order number (and the email used for the order)
here in chat.

## Q: Can I cancel my order after placing it?
A: Orders can be cancelled free of charge as long as they haven't been
dispatched yet. Once an order shows "shipped," it can no longer be
cancelled — you're welcome to return it once it arrives instead.

## Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: At the moment we only ship within Australia. We're evaluating
international shipping for the future.

## Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Afterpay.

## Q: How do I apply a discount or gift card code?
A: Enter your code at checkout in the "Promo code" field before completing
payment. Codes can't be applied retroactively to an order that's already
been placed — contact support if a valid code failed to apply and we can
look into a partial refund of the difference.

## Q: I never received a confirmation email for my order.
A: Please check your spam/junk folder first. If it's still missing, give
support your order number or the email you used, and we can resend the
confirmation or confirm the order went through.
